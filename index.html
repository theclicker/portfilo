About me 01 / 08

Series people
come back for.

I’m James, a creator from Durham in the North East of England. I make videos about the things my generation is actually dealing with: buying a first car, building an office out of an empty room, upgrading a laptop, getting through second year. The series format is what my audience comes back for, and it’s where brands get the most out of working with me.

Alongside the series I post setup and tech videos and everyday clips that regularly pass a million views. A product can be the centre of a multi-part series or a natural part of a normal day. Every sponsored video is scripted, filmed and edited by me, approved by you before it goes live, and followed by a performance report.

“Make the series worth coming back for, and the product comes with it.”
